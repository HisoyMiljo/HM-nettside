Services

Environmental services covering the full decision-making process.

Environmental site investigations

Investigations of soil, groundwater, porewater and excavated materials, linked clearly to remediation needs and regulatory requirements.

Marine environmental investigations

Sediment sampling, receiving-water assessments, marinas, dredging, disposal and consultancy for coastal and port-related matters.

Treatment solutions and in situ remediation

Water treatment, biological and enzymatic degradation, pilot trials and assessment of practical implementation models.

Nature restoration and stormwater

Blue-green solutions, ponds, wetlands, soil conservation and nature-based measures combining function and resilience.

eDNA and environmental monitoring

Targeted monitoring of species and impacts, passive samplers, microplastics and long-term documentation of environmental conditions.

EDD, planning and environmental consultancy

Environmental Due Diligence, environmental support for property and industry, planning processes, disputes and complex decision support.
