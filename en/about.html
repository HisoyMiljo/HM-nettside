About us

Hisøy Miljø - experience-based knowledge for better projects

The company combines substantial senior experience with an agile network of specialists. Our aim is to give clients greater speed, precision and relevance.

Founder

Stein Broch Olsen

Hisøy Miljø AS was founded by Stein Broch Olsen following more than 35 years in senior roles at organisations including HPC, Asplan Viak, Sweco, Rambøll and COWI. He has built environmental teams, led water and environmental initiatives and worked strategically with markets, professional development and service innovation.

His experience ranges from contaminated land and marine sediments to climate adaptation, stormwater, microplastics, environmental monitoring and major complex development and industrial projects.
