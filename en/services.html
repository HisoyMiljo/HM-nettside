Services

Professional support from early clarification to completed measures.

We assemble the right expertise and deliver services at the right level, at the right time and to the highest standard.

Environmental site investigations

Investigations of soil, excavated materials, porewater and groundwater to determine environmental condition, risk and further management.

  • sampling plans and field supervision
  • risk assessment and appraisal of remedial measures
  • soil and materials management and regulatory dialogue
  • environmental support for property development and industry

Marine environmental investigations

Consultancy for ports, marinas, coastal projects and matters where sediments, receiving waters and the marine environment must be properly understood.

  • sediment sampling and classification
  • dredging and disposal assessments
  • marinas, wash water and operational measures
  • restoration of marine ecology - certified marine NiN 3.0

Odour management plans, risk assessment and reduction

Professional support to identify, assess and reduce odour impacts from production facilities, disposal sites and other operations.

  • odour modelling and dispersion assessment
  • odour risk assessments under guideline TA-3019
  • technical and operational measures
  • emission permits from the County Governor in Norway

Treatment solutions and in situ remediation

Practical assessment of treatment technology and on-site measures, from pilot trials through implementation and follow-up.

  • water treatment and process assessment
  • bacterial and enzymatic degradation
  • operational and performance follow-up
  • adaptation to local conditions and project economics

Nature restoration, stormwater and soil conservation

Nature-based solutions that manage water, strengthen natural values and provide robust facilities with low operational vulnerability.

  • ponds, wetlands and open waterways
  • flood routes and blue-green infrastructure
  • soil conservation and terrain adaptation
  • integrating landscape, nature and function

eDNA, microplastics and environmental monitoring

Targeted, modern monitoring of environmental conditions, impacts and biological factors in aquatic environments.

  • eDNA-based investigations
  • microplastics and tracer assessments
  • passive samplers and time-integrated data
  • monitoring before, during and after measures

EDD, planning and strategic consultancy

Environmental support where time, risk and cost must be considered together.

  • Environmental Due Diligence
  • environmental support in planning processes and disputes
  • climate adaptation, sustainability and the EU taxonomy
  • odour emissions and other specialist topics

Sediment traps and passive samplers - rental

Unique design: The sediment traps allow water to pass through a protected volume. Reduced flow velocity captures suspended particles close to the seabed. The traps are currently used around the disposal site at Langøyene in the inner Oslo Fjord, among other locations.
